## What MyGuest helps you manage

MyGuest is a client CRM for hairstylists and independent beauty professionals.
It helps you:

- Store client profiles and notes
- Track appointment history
- Save color-chart and formula details
- Attach optional appointment photos
- Export structured business records

## Common support topics

### Sign-in help

MyGuest supports Sign in with Apple and Google sign-in in the current iPhone
build. If you cannot sign in:

- Confirm you are using the intended account
- Confirm the app is up to date
- Try again on a stable network connection
- Contact support if the issue continues

### Export My Data

`Export My Data` creates a ZIP of CSV files for:

- Clients
- Services
- Appointment logs
- Color-chart data

Exports do not include appointment images.

### Delete Account

`Delete Account` permanently removes hosted account data tied to that account.
If you may need a copy of your structured records later, export your data first.

### Camera and photo-library permissions

MyGuest only uses camera or photo-library access when you choose to attach an
appointment photo. If those permissions are denied, you can continue using the
rest of the app normally.

## Before you contact support

Please include:

- Your device type and iOS version
- Your MyGuest app version and build number
- A short description of what happened
- Screenshots or screen recordings if available
- Whether the issue affects sign-in, exports, account deletion, or photos
