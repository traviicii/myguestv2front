# MyGuest Privacy Policy

Effective date: `[EFFECTIVE_DATE]`

This draft is intended to become the hosted privacy policy URL referenced by the

## 1. Who this policy applies to

This Privacy Policy describes how `[BUSINESS_NAME]` handles information
processed through MyGuest, a client CRM app for hairstylists and similar
independent beauty professionals.

If you use MyGuest, this policy explains what information is stored in the app,
how it is used, what export and deletion controls are available, and how to
contact support.

## 2. Information MyGuest stores

Depending on how you use the app, MyGuest may store:

- Account identity information, such as the email address associated with your
  sign-in
- Profile information you add inside the app
- Client records, including names, phone numbers, email addresses, notes, and
  service preferences
- Appointment history, including service details, prices, notes, and dates
- Color-chart and formula records
- Appointment photos that you choose to attach

MyGuest does not require appointment photos for core functionality. Photos are
optional.

## 3. How MyGuest uses this information

MyGuest uses stored information to:

- Let you sign in securely and access your workspace
- Display client history and appointment details
- Store formulas, color-chart notes, and related business records
- Show overview insights based on the records you create
- Let you export your structured data or delete your account from inside the app

## 4. Photos and device permissions

MyGuest may request access to your camera or photo library only when you choose
to add appointment images.

- Camera access is used to capture an appointment image
- Photo-library access is used to attach an existing image to an appointment

If you deny these permissions, you can still use MyGuest to manage clients,
appointments, and color-chart records.

## 5. Data export

MyGuest includes an `Export My Data` feature.

- Exports are provided as a ZIP containing CSV files
- Exports may include clients, services, appointment logs, and color-chart data
- Exports do not include appointment images

## 6. Account deletion

MyGuest includes in-app account deletion.

If you delete your account, MyGuest is designed to permanently remove hosted
account data associated with that account, including client records, appointment
history, color-chart data, and appointment images. This action cannot be undone.

If you want a copy of your structured records before deletion, use `Export My
Data` first.

## 7. Data sharing and third parties

MyGuest uses third-party sign-in and infrastructure providers to support core
app functionality, including authentication and hosted application services.

As of this draft, the app is not intended to include third-party advertising or
cross-app tracking features. If analytics, crash reporting, or additional
service providers are added later, this policy should be updated before release.

## 8. Data retention

Records remain associated with your account until you edit or remove them, or
until you delete your account. Appointment images remain in MyGuest and are not
included in exports.

## 9. Your choices

You can:

- Review the in-app Data & Privacy section
- Export your structured data
- Decline optional camera and photo-library access
- Delete your account inside the app
